Where the sensitive fields stop

Physical, not preferential. A tablet is issued to one person, but it is read in common areas with other people in line of sight, and it is a vendor device the facility administers. A helper was promised in the first ten seconds that they would never be asked to handle these. The coordinator's desk is neither of those places: they hold the sentence and commitment paperwork and request the birth certificate, so hiding a number they already have protects nobody.

Not assumed away

A person inside cannot verify identity online. The tablet is connected, but only to what is loaded on it: there is no open web to reach a bureau's knowledge-based authentication page, and no camera roll or scanner to answer it with.

A helper outside has no standing until they sign a scoped form. Standing is a record, checked on every request, so a client who cancels from the tablet cuts access off at the next tap.
