Four situations, four different answers

Six answers sort a person into one of four states, and each needs a completely different response. No file at all. A thin or stale file. A damaged file with real debt. Errors on the report.

Only the last carries a legal clock, so it jumps the queue. The credit-invisible path is the fastest of the four.

It fits the plan that already exists

Bridge is one domain inside the Offender Case Plan the coordinator already keeps, not a second system to maintain. Credit goals go into the plan and progress comes back on the quarterly review cadence.

The vital documents packet reentry staff already assemble is the same paperwork a credit bureau asks for, so it gets used rather than collected twice.

Bridge is an independent tool built for reentry programs in New York State. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the New York State Department of Corrections and Community Supervision or any correctional facility.
