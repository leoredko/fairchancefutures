What this build assumes away

Facility and DOC approval, incumbent tablet vendor access, bureau data agreements, funding for the counselor role, and the PII compliance regime a real deployment would sit under. Signed off for class, and said here rather than hidden.

What it does not assume away

A person inside cannot verify identity online, and a helper outside has no standing without a signed form. Those two shape the product, and no waiver makes them disappear. They are enforced in app/surfaces.py and app/authorization.py.
