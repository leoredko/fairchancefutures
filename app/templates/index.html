Bridge

Three roles, three surfaces. Who can do what is set by physics and law, not by preference. That is what splits the app.

Pick a surface. Each one enforces its own limits server-side, so the tablet cannot upload a file even if you go looking for the URL.

Inside

facility tablet

Answer intake, see status, approve what is said about you, cancel a helper's authorization.

Open the tablet

Family

phone, web

Receive the mail, photograph the report, print and mail letters. One task on screen at a time.

Open the helper view

Caseworker

desktop

Triage, read the report, approve letters, hold the deadline across a full caseload.

Open the queue

What this build assumes away

Facility and DOC approval, incumbent tablet vendor access, bureau data agreements, funding for the counselor role, and the PII compliance regime that would govern a real deployment. Signed off for class. It is on a slide rather than hidden.

What it does not assume away

A person inside cannot verify identity online, and a helper outside has no standing without a signed form. Those two shape the product, and no waiver makes them disappear. They are enforced in app/surfaces.py and app/authorization.py.
