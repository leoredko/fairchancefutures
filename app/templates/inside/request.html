There is no test to pass. Online, a bureau asks you questions about old addresses and car loans before it will show you anything. That is a web page, and you cannot reach it. This route is paper, so there is nothing to fail.

How it works

1 You ask. Your coordinator prints the letter below and you sign it.
2 If anything needs to be sworn, the law library has a notary. They have to get you one within 72 hours of asking.
3 It goes to one address in Atlanta and covers Equifax, Experian and TransUnion together.
4 Your file comes back here as mail, in your name. You carry it to your coordinator and you read it on this screen.
